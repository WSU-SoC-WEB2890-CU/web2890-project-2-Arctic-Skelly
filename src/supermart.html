Poof & Dillo Go to the Super Supermart

"Poof & Dillo go to the Super Supermart" is a 3rd person modern mascot platformer adventure, where the titular Poof & Dillo team up to navigate a reimagined grocery store in order to find the Golden Ham!

Drawing influence from both nostalgic 3d platformers in the 1990's, and from the modern day, this game aims to be a timeless call back to anyone who was bored in a grocery store, and let their imagination take over!

After seeing a TV ad for a sweepstakes for a Golden Ham, Poof is eager to get the prize before anyone else! With the help of her Dillo friend, the two will use their unique skills to climb, jump, roll, claw, and crash their way to victory.

Their adventure will take them through 9 unique and larger-than-life levels, such as the "Produce Rainforest," the "Cereal Desert," the "Refrigerated Arctic," and more!

"Poof and Dillo go to the Super Supermart" is expected to be an E for Everyone rated release, and is working to be released on all available platforms for digital purchase in Spring 2028!
