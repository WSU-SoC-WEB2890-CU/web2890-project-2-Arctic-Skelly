End of Time: Belatross

Introduction

"Belatross" is a Trading Card Game between two players, who battle at the end of the world, summoning warriors and relics from the past to fight at the climax at the end of time!

Belatross aims to bridge the gap between "Approachability," and "Nuanced deckbuilding" that many other trading card games seem to struggle with, by simplifying terminology, as well as adapting existing mechanics to keep both players engaged at all times.

Belatross also aims to encourage and cultivate a dedicated community, by hiring artists from within the community itself, and even turning some users into cameos with their own cards!

Belatross aims to reach full production by Winter 2026!

Here's How To Play!

This card game takes influences from games like "Magic: The Gathering" as well as "Yu-Gi-Oh," but intends to be easier to learn and approach, like "Hearthstone." To play you will need the following...

  • A deck of 30-60 cards
  • 10 tokens to use as "Summoner points"
  • An opponent with their own deck and tokens

Decide on who goes first. Each player draws 7 cards from the top of their decks. At the start of your turn, draw a card from your deck. At the start of a player's turn, they receive 5 "Summoner Points." These are your currency to play cards.

Each card costs a certain amount of Summoner Points, with more powerful cards costing more. Any unspent Summoner points carry over to your next turn. You can have up to a total of 10 points.

Card Anatomy

  1. Card Title/Name
  2. Point cost
  3. Element Affiliation: Elements are Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Solar, and Psychic
  4. Card Keywords, used to categorize card attributes
  5. DEF Value: How much ATK value is needed to remove this card
  6. ATK Value: How much this card can attack for each turn
  7. Ability Description: Card rule that remains active until card is removed
  8. Flavor text quote

There are 3 card types:

Warrior

  • Character cards
  • Primary form of damage and defense
  • Always come with an ATK and DEF value
  • DEF value is the required total ATK value required to remove the card in one turn.
  • Opponent may combine ATK values of any active warrior to "team up" against a single card with high DEF value.

Trap

  • Situational card that has a negative effect on the opponent
  • Traps are placed face down hidden from the opponent's view
  • Each card has certain conditions in order to take effect.
  • Opponent may target any trap card to destroy it, though that may activate the trap instead.
  • Unless otherwise stated, a trap card has a DEF value of 1, and can be easily destroyed.

Item

  • Item cards that grant the player a positive effect.
  • Items are either "Summoned" or "Equipped."
  • Equipped items must be attached to a Warrior card in play.
  • If a warrior card with a item is defeated, the item card is removed as well.

Each player turn involves:

  • Refreshing your Summoner's Tokens
  • Drawing a card
  • Playing however many cards you wish
  • Attacking with your active cards
  • The turn ALWAYS ends with attacking.

Players continue back and forth with their turns in like manner. If the opponent has no active cards in play during your turn, you may attack them directly. Summoner health is decided on beforehand and is relevant to game length.

Victory is achieved when the opponent either runs out of Summoner health, or runs out of cards to play.
